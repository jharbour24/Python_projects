# The SAG → Netflix natural experiment — what it tells the Tonys

_Generated 2026-06-08 19:26._

**Bottom line up front.** SAG was dying on linear like every award show. Moving to Netflix did **not** rescue it into a phenomenon (it failed to crack Netflix's weekly Top 10 in 2024) — but it also did not obviously shrink its nominal reach, and its streaming views are *growing*. The honest lesson for the Tonys: **streaming is a global-reach + younger-demo + on-demand-discovery play, not a relevance cure.** The relevance problem is upstream in the content (see DEEPER_ANALYSIS Q2), not in the pipe.

## 2. The move broke the ruler (why you can't just difference it)

From 2023 the number stops being Nielsen average-minute viewers and becomes Netflix/YouTube **views** — a different, generally larger unit. **These are not comparable.** Reported streaming era:

| SAG year | Platform | Reported *views* (M) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | Netflix/YouTube | 1.50 | Bridge year: free livestream on Netflix's YouTube; ~1.1–1.5M views across YT/FB/X in first 12–24h. Not Nielsen-comparable. |
| 2024 | Netflix | 1.80 | First under the Netflix multi-year deal; 1.8M views — 'on par' with 2022's last linear outing, but did NOT crack Netflix Top 10 (<2.1M in 24h). |
| 2025 | Netflix | 2.60 | 2.6M views over the week (No. 7 on Netflix weekly list); grew ~44% vs 2024. Rebranding toward 'Actor Awards'. |

Within the streaming era (a clean, like-for-like comparison), SAG views grew **1.8M → 2.6M (+44%)**. But note the ceiling: in 2024 SAG **failed to make Netflix's weekly Top 10**, i.e. it remained a *minor* title on the platform.

## 3. Bracketing the move (counterfactual vs. reported)

- **Counterfactual linear floor** — extrapolating SAG's 2013–2022 linear trend forward: it would have drawn ≈ **1.4M (2024)** and **1.2M (2025)**, and falling.
- **Reported Netflix views**: 1.8M (2024), 2.6M (2025), and rising.
- **Read with care:** the Netflix figure is nominally higher *and* a measurement-inflated unit, so the most you can honestly say is the move **plausibly held or modestly grew nominal reach while trading a shrinking, measurable linear audience for a growing-but-fuzzy global, on-demand one.** It did not manufacture cultural heat.

## 4. Read-across: what this says about the Tonys' post-2026 home

| Factor | SAG (pre-move) | Tonys (now) | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Last linear audience | 1.8M (2022) | 4.85M (2025) | Tonys are **~2.7× larger** on linear — **more to lose** by abandoning it outright |
| Audience age | mid-tier | **oldest of any award show (~61)** | Tonys have the **most demographic upside** from streaming's younger reach |
| Global audience fit | US-centric | **~41% of Broadway ticket buyers are international** | A global streamer aligns broadcast reach with the actual customer base |
| Platform outcome | minor Netflix title (no Top 10) | — | Streaming **won't** make the Tonys a phenomenon on its own |

**Recommendation logic (not a verdict — a framework).** The SAG case argues *against* a pure linear→streaming swap and *for* a **hybrid**: keep a linear/free broadcast spine to protect the still-meaningful (if old, shrinking) ~5M linear audience, while adding **global, on-demand, ad-supported streaming** (Netflix-style reach or free YouTube) to fix the two things linear can't: the **age skew** and the **international mismatch**. Crucially, expect streaming to **relocate** the Tonys, not **revive** them — revival has to come from the shows themselves (Phase 2, Q2).

## Caveats

- **Single treated unit, broken measurement.** This is a structured case study, not a clean DiD; the regime change forbids a simple treatment effect on viewership.
- **Mid-decade SAG linear figures vary by source** (2015–2017 especially) and are flagged low-confidence; the *trend* is robust, individual cells less so.
- **The decisive variables are still missing:** Netflix has not released SAG age/demographic splits, and we have no platform-neutral attention series (Google Trends / Wikipedia pageviews) yet. Backfilling those — and Netflix demo data if obtainable — would convert this from a directional case study into a quantified treatment effect. That is the top next step.
